---
title: "2. TypeScript Essentials for LLD"
---

You already write TypeScript every day in NestJS and React. This chapter is not a full language tour. It covers the parts of TypeScript that decide whether your class design looks clean and deliberate in a 60–90 minute machine-coding or LLD round. Every idea here comes back in later chapters: in SOLID, in design patterns, and in the case studies (order book, wallet, notification service, parking lot).

**What you'll learn**

- When to reach for `type` and when to reach for `interface`, and why the interviewer may ask
- How structural typing can bite you (mixing up an `OrderId` and a `UserId`) and how branded types fix it
- Classes in depth: parameter properties, getters/setters, static members, `private` vs `#private`, `readonly`
- Abstract classes vs interfaces, `implements` with several interfaces, and the `override` keyword
- Generics for reusable building blocks like `Repository<T>`
- Enums vs string-literal unions, and why most TS teams now pick unions
- Discriminated unions with an exhaustive `switch` and `never` for order state machines
- Type guards, utility types, overloads, `Map`/`Set` stores, custom errors, `unknown`, `satisfies`
- How to handle money safely (integer paise and `bigint`), which matters a lot at a broker like Angel One
- A checklist of TS features for every LLD round, and the mistakes that cost candidates marks
