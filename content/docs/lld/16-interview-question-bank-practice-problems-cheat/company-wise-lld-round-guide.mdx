## What almost every company grades

Pulled together from all the write-ups, in rough order of how often they come up:

1. **It runs.** Flipkart ("should be running"), Uber ("demoable"), Groww (demo on their examples), CRED (tests pass).
2. **You clarified scope** before coding (Razorpay, Salesforce, Meesho).
3. **Modularity**: models, services and repositories kept apart (Flipkart, PhonePe, Razorpay, CRED).
4. **Extensibility**: a new type or strategy without rewriting (Flipkart, Razorpay, Walmart, Swiggy).
5. **Patterns used for a reason** (PhonePe's Command feedback; Strategy and Observer at Walmart and Salesforce).
6. **Readability and naming.**
7. **Validation, exceptions and edge cases.** Happy-path-only code is a known failure.
8. **Concurrency** (Uber's core topic; PhonePe, Walmart, Salesforce, Meesho follow-ups).
9. **Tests** (Atlassian, CRED, PhonePe).
10. **Communication**: talk while you code; explain it in the walkthrough.
11. **Speed**: a clean subset beats a buggy whole.
12. **Data-structure choice and complexity** (Groww, Google).
13. **In-memory storage**, ideally behind a repository interface.
