This last chapter is the one you will keep open in a tab during your preparation. The earlier chapters taught the ideas and solved the core problems. This chapter pulls everything together into one list you can work through:

- **what gets asked**: the master list, the top 35, and a company-by-company guide;
- **what you will be quizzed on**: 60+ concept questions and 15 "what if" follow-ups, with model answers;
- **what to practise on your own**: 30 problems this book deliberately does not solve;
- **what to glance at on the morning of the interview**: cheat sheets;
- **how to spend the next 12 weeks, or the next 2**.

A note on honesty before we start. The problem and company data comes from public interview write-ups collected in September 2026. Some of those pages (LeetCode Discuss, Medium) could only be seen as search-result summaries, so their details are marked **[snippet]**. Where a detail is a reasonable guess rather than something reported, it is marked **(inferred)**. Keep those markers in mind: "Angel One asked a leaderboard" is a snippet-level signal, not a guarantee. Use this data to choose what to practise, not to predict your exact question.

**How to read the chapter references.** "§9 Parking Lot" means the Parking Lot case study in Chapter 9. "§7.8" points at a numbered section. "Practice" means the book does not solve it. Some of those problems appear in §16.6 with requirements and hints, so you can solve them yourself.
