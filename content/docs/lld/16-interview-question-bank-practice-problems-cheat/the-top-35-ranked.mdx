The ranking mostly follows how many sources and companies mention each problem. I then moved problems up where they matter more for you: Indian fintech and broking companies, and the SDE-2 focus on concurrency. The "what they test" column says what the interviewer is really checking, which is usually not the headline feature.

| Rank | Problem | Level | What they test | Book |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parking Lot | B | Can you break a vague domain into entities, and add a vehicle type or pricing rule without editing old classes (Strategy + Factory)? | §9 |

| 3 | Rate Limiter | I | Correct window arithmetic, a Strategy per resource, and whether you lock per key or globally. | §10, §7.9 |
