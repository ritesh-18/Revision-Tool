Level legend: **B** = Basic (SDE-1 warm-up, mostly OOP modelling), **I** = Intermediate (typical SDE-1/SDE-2 machine coding), **S2** = SDE-2 (concurrency, scheduling, trading or money correctness). A year in brackets such as ('26) is the year of the report where one was known. CodeZym's 2026 company lists are cited as ('26) even though individual questions in them are not dated.
