## A closing word

You now have everything this book can give you: the language, the principles, the patterns, the method, around forty solved systems, and a bank of questions and problems to practise on. The rest is repetitions. Build a problem, time it, review it honestly, and build the next one. By the eighth or ninth timed build, the entities, seams and edge cases start to come to you without effort — and that is what the interviewer is looking for. Good luck, Ritesh.
