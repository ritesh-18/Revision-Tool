---
title: "3. OOP, SOLID & Design Principles"
---

Machine-coding and LLD rounds are rarely about clever algorithms. The interviewer is watching whether your classes have clear jobs, whether a new requirement lands in one place or ten, and whether you can *explain* why you split things the way you did. This chapter gives you the vocabulary and the reflexes for that.

**What you'll learn**

- The four OOP pillars in TypeScript, and when each one helps or quietly hurts
- All five SOLID principles, each with a broken design, a refactor that runs, how to spot the violation, and a short interview answer
- DRY, KISS and YAGNI, plus the over-engineering traps that cost people machine-coding rounds
- Composition over inheritance, the Law of Demeter, cohesion and coupling
- When to pick an `interface` and when to pick an `abstract class`
- The six object relationships, UML class-diagram notation and sequence diagrams in Mermaid
- How to turn a requirements paragraph into classes with noun/verb analysis
- A checklist of design smells and how to refactor each one
