---
title: "Domain primer: just enough stock-market vocabulary"
---

You don't need to be a trader. You do need to use these words correctly, because interviewers at brokers notice quickly when a candidate doesn't.

| Term | Meaning | Why it matters in code |
|------|---------|------------------------|
| **Bid / Ask** | A *bid* is a buy order waiting in the book. An *ask* (offer) is a waiting sell. The best bid is the highest bid price and the best ask is the lowest ask price. | Bids need a max-first structure and asks need a min-first structure. |
| **Spread** | Best ask minus best bid. | If best bid ≥ best ask, the book is "crossed" and a trade should already have happened. |
| **Limit order** | "Buy 10 INFY at ₹1,500.00 **or better**." A buy fills at ≤ its limit and a sell at ≥ its limit. Anything left over waits in the book. | Needs a price, and that price must be valid. |
| **Market order** | "Buy 10 INFY at whatever the best available price is, right now." | Has no price. It never rests in the book: whatever doesn't fill is cancelled. |
| **Price-time priority** | Better price first. At the same price, the order that arrived earlier goes first (FIFO). | Keep a sorted set of price levels, with a FIFO queue at each level. |
| **Trade price** | When an incoming order matches a resting one, the trade happens at the **resting (maker) order's price**. | A buyer who bids ₹2,505 against an ask at ₹2,500 pays ₹2,500. |
| **Partial fill** | Only part of the quantity is matched. The rest stays open, or is cancelled if the order is market/IOC. | Track `filledQty` and `remaining`, and use status `PARTIALLY_FILLED`. |
| **IOC** | Immediate-Or-Cancel: fill whatever you can right now and cancel the rest. | A time-in-force flag checked after matching. |
| **Tick size** | The smallest allowed price step, e.g. ₹0.05 for many NSE equities. The exact tick depends on the instrument and price band, so treat it as data. | `price % tickSize === 0` in paise. |
| **Lot size** | The minimum tradable quantity multiple. It is 1 for cash equities. F&O contracts come in lots defined by the exchange, and those lot sizes change over time. | `qty % lotSize === 0`. |
| **Margin / blocked funds** | Money set aside when you place an order so you can't spend it twice. | Keep `available` and `blocked` as separate balances. |
| **Holdings vs positions** | *Holdings* are shares delivered to your demat account (delivery/CNC trades from earlier days). *Positions* are today's open trades, whether intraday or not yet settled. | Two different views, updated from the same fills. |
| **Average price** | Weighted cost of what you hold: total cost ÷ quantity. | Store total cost in paise and derive the average, so rounding errors don't pile up. |
| **LTP** | Last traded price. | Drives P&L, alerts and stop-loss triggers. |

All code in this chapter stores money as **integer paise** (`₹2,500.05` becomes `250005`). Floats cannot represent 0.05 exactly, and an order book that compares `2500.05 === 2500.0499999` will eventually mismatch.
