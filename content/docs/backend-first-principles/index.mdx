These are my own study notes for the [Backend from First Principles](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLui3EUkuMTPgZcV0QhQrOcwMPcBCcd_Q1) series by **Sriniously**. Each page follows its lesson's structure and explains every idea at the engineering level: the problem, why it happens, the mechanism that solves it, the implementation, and edge cases.

<Callout type="info">
  - 🎓 **Lesson**: what the video teaches, in my own words (not a transcript).
  - 🔧 **Going deeper**: extra code, edge cases and documentation facts added on top of a topic the lesson covers.
  - ➕ **Beyond the lesson**: a topic the video does not cover at all, added because a backend engineer should know it.

  Watch the lesson, then use the page to revise. Edit anything that differs from the video.
</Callout>

## How a backend fits together

<Mermaid chart={`flowchart LR
  C[Client] -->|HTTP| G[Routing]
  G --> M["Middleware: auth, validation"]
  M --> CT[Controllers]
  CT --> S[Services]
  S --> R[Repositories]
  R --> DB[(Postgres)]
  S --> CA[(Cache)]
  S --> Q[[Task queue]]
  Q --> W[Workers]
  S --> OS[(Object storage)]
  W --> OS
  S --> SE[(Search index)]
  S -. events .-> RT["Real-time / webhooks"]`} />
